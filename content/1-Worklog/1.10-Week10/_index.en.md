## Objectives

- Understand the purpose of Amazon Relational Database Service.
- Study the basic components of an RDS configuration.

## Learning summary

- Understood the role of RDS in managing relational databases.
- Identified database engines, instance classes and storage as configuration components.
- Understood the purposes of endpoints and ports.
- Connected VPC, subnet group and Security Group concepts to database networking.
- Distinguished network access from database authentication and authorization.
- Learned the purposes of automated backups and snapshots.
- Distinguished the main availability purpose of Multi-AZ from the read scaling purpose of Read Replicas.
- Studied sample configurations without creating or connecting to a DB instance.

This week's reading helped me understand database configuration, connectivity and data protection and distinguish recovery from availability and read scaling.
