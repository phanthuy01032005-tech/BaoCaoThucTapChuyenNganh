## Reflections on the learning process

Studying First Cloud AI Journey materials helped me approach AWS topics in sequence, from account and cost management to permissions, networking, compute, storage and databases.

Maintaining a weekly worklog helped me divide the material into manageable topics, track my progress and identify concepts requiring further review. This showed me the value of clear objectives and regular note-taking.

## Helpful aspects of the materials

- The topics introduce several groups of AWS services.
- Diagrams help beginners understand relationships between components.
- Sample configurations support technical explanations.
- Security and cost management topics encourage responsible resource use.

## Learning difficulties

Networking, IAM policies and container configuration required repeated reading. With limited practical experience, I sometimes found it difficult to visualize how all the components work together.

English documentation required additional vocabulary research. Since service information can change, learners need to check official documentation before applying instructions.

## Suggestions for the materials

- Include a Vietnamese–English glossary for each topic.
- State prerequisite knowledge at the beginning of each lesson.
- Add review questions and explanations of commonly confused concepts.
- Indicate update dates and changes affecting instructions.
- For practical exercises, identify resources, permissions, potential costs and cleanup steps.
- Include common error scenarios and guidance on researching their causes.

## Suggestions for learning support

I would welcome more beginner-focused discussions explaining diagrams, terminology and documentation reading strategies. Topic-based question-and-answer sessions could help learners consolidate their knowledge.

For practical sessions, I suggest small exercises with clear objectives and cost controls. Learners could then summarize their results and discuss remaining questions.
