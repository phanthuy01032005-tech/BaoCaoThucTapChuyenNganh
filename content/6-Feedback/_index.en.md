## Overall evaluation

### 1. Learning environment during the internship

The internship provided an opportunity to explore cloud computing and develop a more organized approach to learning. Through First Cloud AI Journey materials, I studied AWS services in sequence, starting with foundational concepts and progressing to related topics.

Maintaining a worklog over 12 weeks helped me identify reading objectives, track my progress and review concepts that required further clarification.

### 2. Learning materials and guidance

The materials provided a foundation for selecting topics and organizing my study. Diagrams, instructions and sample configurations helped me understand the purpose of individual components.

For beginners, clearly stated prerequisites and learning outcomes would make independent study easier to follow.

### 3. Relevance to my major

The learning topics were relevant to Information Technology, particularly computer networking, operating systems, databases and security.

Studying IAM, VPC, EC2 and RDS helped me connect university knowledge with how resources are organized in a cloud environment.

### 4. Opportunities to develop skills

In addition to AWS knowledge, I practised reading technical documentation, researching terminology, taking notes and preparing reports.

Preparing content in Vietnamese and English also encouraged me to use technical terms carefully and present information consistently.

### 5. Community learning and exchange

Learning about the First Cloud AI Journey community helped me recognize the value of sharing knowledge and learning from people with similar interests.

I would welcome more beginner-focused discussions explaining concepts, diagrams and topics that are easy to confuse.

### 6. Support for independent study

For documentation-based learning, useful support includes a clear learning path, accessible references and a channel for questions.

Glossaries, review questions and service updates would help learners research topics and check their understanding.

## Additional questions

### What was the most satisfying part of the internship?

The most satisfying part was developing a weekly AWS learning plan. Rather than studying individual services in isolation, I began connecting topics involving identities, networking, compute, storage and databases.

Keeping a worklog also helped me review what I had studied and identify areas that needed further attention.

### What could be improved for future interns?

An introductory guide could explain the prerequisite knowledge, documentation reading strategies and ways to record learning outcomes.

Topic-based question-and-answer sessions and review questions after each lesson could help learners identify misunderstandings earlier.

### Would you recommend the program to friends? Why?

I would recommend the program to students interested in cloud computing who are willing to study independently.

The learning materials provide a structured introduction to AWS. Learners still need to set aside time for reading, note-taking and further research to meet their personal goals.

## Suggestions and future expectations

### What would improve the learning experience?

- State the objectives and prerequisites for each topic.
- Include Vietnamese–English glossaries and summary diagrams.
- Add review questions and explanations of commonly confused concepts.
- Clearly indicate update dates and changes affecting instructions.
- Organize question-and-answer sessions around related topics.
- For practical exercises, explain permissions, potential costs and resource cleanup.

### Would you like to continue learning in the future?

Yes. I would like to strengthen my AWS knowledge and gain practical experience.

My next step is to begin with small exercises, define clear objectives and control costs. I plan to record the results and compare them with the concepts studied in the documentation.

### Additional reflections

This internship helped me recognize that learning technology requires persistence, independent research and a habit of checking information.

I would like to thank the internship organization, mentors and the First Cloud AI Journey community for the opportunity to explore AWS. I will continue developing my knowledge and skills to prepare for future work. a foundation for continued learning, practical experience and professional development.
